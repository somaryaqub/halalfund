Halal Affordable Housing Investment Fund

Prepared for the Islamic Development Bank (Awqaf Investments) by IslamicFamily and SVX Canada. Project: Diamond at Dunluce, 365 Dunluce Road NW, Edmonton, Alberta, Canada.
Rendering of Diamond at Dunluce
The Ask
$15 million in equity or halal debt

IslamicFamily and SVX Canada invite IsDB to participate in a Shariah-compliant fund that will purchase a purpose-built, family-sized affordable housing project in Canada. We are flexible on instrument, size, phasing and terms.

~$20MFund raise
$37.6MProject cost
$12.9MGrants confirmed (34%)

The Fund raises $5.67M by 2028 and ~$15M in 2031. The project is 96 homes and 290 bedrooms, with 72 homes below market rent. Groundbreaking is complete.

1Why this fits IsDB

  • Muslim communities in non-member countries. IsDB's mandate includes them. This is a community-led, institutionally governed response to a documented housing need: Muslim Canadians face a core housing need rate of 11%, against 4.9% nationally, and families wait 3 to 4 years for a family-sized affordable unit.
  • Perpetual community benefit. After investor distributions, residual proceeds return to IslamicFamily for charitable purposes in perpetuity, a structure that resonates with waqf principles.
  • A replicable Islamic finance model. Canada's first institutional-quality halal housing fund, built on Musharakah with no interest-bearing instruments at any level of the capital stack.
  • Catalytic capital. Confirmed government grants already cover 34% of project cost, so IsDB's capital sits alongside substantial public funding.
  • Development impact. About 1,740 people housed over 20 years, aligned with SDG 1 (No Poverty) and SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities), with annual impact reporting to investors. See the figures.

3Structure and participation

The Fund is a GP/LP limited partnership. The General Partner is owned 60% by IslamicFamily and 40% by SVX Canada. IsDB can participate in the way that best fits its mandate:

  • Equity. Class 1 (projected return ~3.5% above return of capital) or Class 0 (capital return only, for concessional investors).
  • Halal debt. Murabaha, Ijara or a similar structure, in place of or alongside equity, modelled at the same ~3.5% projected return.
  • Flexibility. We are open to discussing ticket size, phasing, tenor, reporting, and the level of IsDB's engagement in governance.

Full terms and investor classes are in Annex A3.

4Projected return

Impact comes first. Diamond at Dunluce is built to house families, and the return follows from that mission. Over 20 years the project is projected to house about 1,740 people. See the impact figures.

~3.5%Projected annual return, current pro forma

The projected return is currently ~3.5%. It is based on a very conservative pro forma that prioritizes community impact and affordability. The model is rigorous and defensible, and our goal is to under-promise and over-deliver.

Built for stability

The project

Purpose-built family homes with multi-year waitlists. The model assumes 72 of 96 homes below market rent and 4% vacancy, and Year 1 income covers financing costs 1.64 times. Confirmed grants already fund 34% of cost.

The fund

Managed by SVX Canada with institutional governance and reporting. Returns come from a revenue share tied to a real asset, investors are repaid before surplus returns to charity, and investors can exit if targets are not met.

The Canadian economy

A stable G7 economy with strong legal protections for investors and sustained public commitment to affordable housing, shown by confirmed City and provincial grants and a federal application advancing.

Projected returns are not guarantees. They come from a revenue-share structure tied to the performance of the asset. The full pro forma is available to IsDB's team on request.

5Shariah Leadership

This Fund goes beyond mere compliance. From the very start it has been built on Shariah principles, so Shariah is its foundation and not a review applied at the end. Returns come from the real performance of the asset under a Musharakah partnership, and no interest-bearing instruments are used at any level of the capital stack.

Shariah from day one

The structure, contracts and governance were designed around Shariah principles from the first draft.

Institutional quality

Canada's first institutional-quality Shariah-based fund, with professional fund management, reporting and governance.

A door for mainstream capital

It opens the door for mainstream institutional investment into Shariah portfolios.

The Fund's contracts and agreements are being verified by Mufti Mirza Zain Baig, an AAOIFI-certified scholar, and review is in its final stages. Mufti Mirza Zain is available to speak directly with IsDB's Shariah team.

6Governance and management

SVX Canada is our governance and management lead, bringing exceptional governance and visibility to investors. It is a nonprofit financial services firm with $48.4M in assets under management, 527 investors, and over $218M mobilized for affordable housing across more than a dozen funds. SVX co-owns the General Partner and is the contracted fund manager.

IslamicFamily is a registered charity in Edmonton and an Imagine Canada accredited organization, with four decades of community service. It operates the housing. Legal counsel is Pallett Valo LLP.
