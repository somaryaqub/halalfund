How the numbers are calculated

Capital stack

Equity (fixed)
= Total project cost × 40%
= $37,539,270 × 0.40 = $15,015,708
Halal raise
= Total − Equity − City − AHPP − BCH − Phil − Loan
Base: $37,539,270 − $15,016k − $3,582k − $6,454k − $4,000k = $8,487,089
Total raise
= Halal raise + Owner equity
= $8,487,089 + $15,015,708 = $23,502,797

Returns

Annual financing cost
= Total raise × Offered rate
= $23,502,797 × 2.75% = $646,327/yr
Max supportable rate
= Effective NOI ÷ Total raise
Effective NOI = Year 1 NOI + Zakat (0% loan has no annual cash impact — refinanced at maturity)
= $849,845 ÷ $23,502,797 = 3.62%
Debt coverage ratio (DCR)
= Year 1 NOI ÷ Annual financing cost
= $849,845 ÷ $646,327 = 1.31x
Key assumptions: Year 1 NOI = $849,845 (proforma revenue $1,469,884 − operating expenses $620,040). Equity = 40% of total project cost, fixed. Both tranches (Halal financing + owner equity buyback) earn the same offered rate. Philanthropic gifts are permanent capital — they reduce the Halal raise with no repayment obligation, leaving full NOI available for investors. 0% loans reduce the Halal raise with no annual cash impact — they are refinanced at maturity (year 5 or 10), the same way Halal financing is handled. The toggle shows when the refinance obligation falls due. Zakat is operating support only; it increases NOI but does not reduce the raise.
